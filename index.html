The track

Option B · Into the dark

Look up, Sebi.

One of the darkest skies in Germany, and a person who knows every light in it.

A guided night in the Sternenpark Westhavelland — Germany's first Dark Sky Park, an hour and a half west of Berlin, with an astronomer who has been doing this for longer than you've been alive.

Options

The place

Sternenpark Westhavelland

  • WhatGermany's first International Dark Sky Park, certified in February 2014. 1,380 km² of Brandenburg where they deliberately keep the lights off.
  • How darkBortle class 3 — about as dark as it gets in Germany. The village of Gülpe is routinely called the darkest place in the country. On a clear moonless night the Milky Way is bright enough to cast a shadow. Yours.
  • WhereHavelland, ~75 km west of Berlin. Roughly 1.5 h by car via the B5. The good spots are around Gülpe, Parey and Kleßen; the Naturpark HQ in Parey has an observation platform and a small lecture room.
  • WhoThomas Becker — hobby astronomer for longer than you've been alive. He runs the observation evenings in the Astronomie-Labor Kleßen, with four telescopes including a 600 mm Dobsonian. That's a telescope you could climb into.
  • Moresternenpark-westhavelland.de ↗ · the observation evenings ↗
